# Privacy Policy for WordWeave

**Last updated:** October 16, 2026

Welcome to **WordWeave** ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application handles user information.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### A. Personal Information
**WordWeave does NOT collect, store, or transmit any Personally Identifiable Information (PII).**
- We do not require you to create an account, register, or log in.
- We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, or precise physical location.

### B. Game Progress & Local Data
All your gameplay progress (cleared levels, stars, theme preferences, and hints used) is saved **strictly locally on your device** using local offline storage (`AsyncStorage`). This data never leaves your device and is never uploaded to any remote server.

### C. Automatically Collected Data (Third-Party Services)
To support development and provide free hints, WordWeave integrates **Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK)** for in-game advertisements (Banner, Interstitial, and Rewarded Video Ads). 

Google AdMob may collect and process certain non-personally identifiable information automatically, such as:
- **Device Identifiers**: Google Advertising ID (AAID)
- **Technical Information**: Device model, operating system version, screen resolution, and mobile carrier
- **Diagnostic & Performance Data**: App crash logs and ad interaction statistics (impressions, clicks) to prevent fraud and measure ad delivery.

For more information on how Google processes and protects this data, please consult Google's Privacy & Terms:
- [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites)

---

## 2. Permissions Used by the App

WordWeave requests only the minimum necessary Android permissions to function properly:
- `INTERNET`: Required to fetch and display ads from Google AdMob and check for game updates.
- `VIBRATE`: Used solely for subtle tactile haptic feedback during gameplay (e.g., when tracing words or solving puzzles).

We do **NOT** request access to sensitive permissions such as Camera, Microphone, Storage/Photos, Contacts, or GPS Location.

---

## 3. Network Security

WordWeave enforces strict transport layer security (`android:usesCleartextTraffic="false"`). All network communications are encrypted via industry-standard HTTPS.

---

## 4. Children’s Privacy

WordWeave is designed as a family-friendly word puzzle game suitable for general audiences of all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13 (or under the applicable age of consent in your jurisdiction). In accordance with Google AdMob policies, ads served within WordWeave are configured to comply with family-safe rating standards (Max Content Rating: G).

---

## 5. Opt-Out of Personalized Ads

You can opt out of personalized advertisements or reset your Advertising ID at any time directly through your Android device settings:
- Open **Settings** on your Android device.
- Navigate to **Google** > **All services** > **Ads**.
- Tap **Delete advertising ID** or toggle **Opt out of Ads Personalization**.

Even if you opt out, ads may still be shown in the app, but they will be contextual rather than personalized.
